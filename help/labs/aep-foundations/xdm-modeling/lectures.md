XDM (Experience Data Model) is Adobe’s standardized framework for organizing customer experience data across different systems. It ensures that data from various sources—like websites, apps, and CRMs—is structured consistently, making it easier to unify, analyze, and activate in real time. This consistency is crucial for building accurate customer profiles, enabling personalized experiences, and supporting AI-driven insights across Adobe Experience Platform.
