# Lectures

In Adobe Experience Platform, there are two main patterns of data ingestion: batch and streaming. Batch ingestion involves uploading large volumes of data at scheduled intervals, while streaming ingestion brings in data in real time as events happen. Understanding these patterns is important because it helps businesses choose the right speed and method for bringing data into the platform—ensuring that marketing experiences are timely, relevant, and aligned with business needs.
