## Why content creation matters in AJO

Content is at the centre of every customer interaction. In Adobe Journey Optimizer, creating high-quality, brand-aligned content enables:

- A consistent customer experience across all channels
- Faster campaign execution using reusable templates and fragments
- Compliance with brand standards and legal requirements
- Personalisation at scale to drive engagement and results

## Key concepts

This lab introduces the core elements required to create and manage branded content for Connection 5G.

### 1. Brands

A Brand in AJO represents a unique identity (for example, Connection 5G). Each brand includes:

- Visual identity
- Writing style
- Tone and voice rules
- Assets and creative materials

### 2. Brand guidelines

Brand Guidelines define:

- Writing style and tone
- Voice rules
- Legal requirements
- Visual standards (colour, imagery, iconography)

### 3. Journeys

Journeys are automated workflows that orchestrate customer experiences based on:

- Profile attributes
- Events or triggers
- Eligibility and conditions

### 4. Templates

Templates are reusable structures for channels, for example:

- Email
- SMS
- Push notifications
- In-app messages
