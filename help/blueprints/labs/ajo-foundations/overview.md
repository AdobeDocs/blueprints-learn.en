# Overview

## What you learn

1. **Adobe Journey Optimizer Architecture & Capabilities:**
   - Understand how AJO is built natively on Adobe Experience Platform and its place among personalization tools
   - Explore the core capabilities that power orchestrated, real-time customer journeys
1. **Data Stores:**
   - Differentiate between the Real-Time Customer Profile and the Relational Store within AJO
   - Hands-on practice configuring and working with both data stores
1. **Orchestrated Campaigns:**
   - Learn the technical building blocks behind message delivery and channel configuration
   - Build and launch an orchestrated campaign, including configuring email channels
1. **Journeys and Decisioning:**
   - Understand the technical building blocks of Journeys and how they use the Real-Time Customer Profile
   - Learn how Experience Edge and edge decisioning enable real-time, in-session personalization
   - Explore decision policies, ranking, and content authoring with AI



## Target audience

- Solution Architects
- Enterprise Architects
- Marketers and Campaign Managers
- Developers
- Journey Orchestration Specialists



## Recommended experience or familiarity

- Journey orchestration or campaign management concepts
- General understanding of Adobe Experience Platform and the Real-Time Customer Profile
- Working with REST APIs
- Email and cross-channel messaging concepts
- Exposure to CDPs, marketing automation, or other martech platforms


## Prerequisites

If you're working through this bootcamp at your own pace, complete [Postman setup](setup.md#postman-setup) before starting the labs. **Configure email channels** and its dependent labs need a subdomain delegated to Adobe, and **Flagship phone launch** needs SMS credentials. See [Setup](setup.md) for details.
