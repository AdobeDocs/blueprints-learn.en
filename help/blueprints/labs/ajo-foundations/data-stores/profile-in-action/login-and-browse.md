# Login & browse

## Login via UI

1. Navigate to [https://experience.adobe.com](https://experience.adobe.com/) in your browser.
1. Log in using the Adobe ID that has developer access to your sandbox — the same one you used to complete [Developer Console Setup](../../sandbox-setup/developer-console-setup.md).
1. On the **Select an account** screen choose the **Company or School Account**.

![Adobe Experience Platform login page](assets/login-and-browse-adobe-login-page.png "Adobe Login page")

![Prompt to select the Company or School Account profile](assets/login-and-browse-select-account-profile-prompt.png "Adobe Login with prompt for profle selection")

![Adobe Experience Platform password entry page](assets/login-and-browse-adobe-password-page.png "Adobe Password page")



## Launch Experience Platform

Click on the Experience Platform icon from the quick access panel to access your bootcamp sandbox

>[!NOTE]
>
>You may not see this screen and be launched directly into the Experience Platform.  If so skip this step.

![Quick access panel with the Experience Platform icon](assets/login-and-browse-quick-access-panel.png "Quick access for the Experience Platform")
